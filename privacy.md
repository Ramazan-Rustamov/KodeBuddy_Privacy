# KodeBuddy Privacy Policy

_Effective date: October 26, 2026_

KodeBuddy is a coding-practice app for iPhone and iPad, made by Ramazan Rustamov ("I", "me"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: **I don't collect your personal data, and the app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.**

## What stays on your device

Your settings (language, difficulty, preferences) and your learning progress (topics, XP, mistakes, results) are stored in the app on your device.

## iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, KodeBuddy syncs your progress and settings between your own devices through **your private iCloud account**. This data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account. I can't see or access it. You can turn this off in Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

## Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, KodeBuddy uses it for leaderboards, achievements and duels:

- your Game Center nickname, scores and achievements are shown to other players as Game Center allows;
- with your permission, the app reads your Game Center friends list so you can challenge friends;
- in a duel, your answers and score are sent to your opponent through Game Center.

Game Center is provided by Apple, and Apple's privacy policy applies to it. I don't receive or store this information on any server of my own.

## Subscriptions

KodeBuddy Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; I never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether your subscription is active. You can manage or cancel it in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

## Feedback

If you tap **Send feedback**, the app opens your own email app with a draft that includes the app version, iOS version, device model and the course you're on, to help me fix problems. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself. I use your email only to reply to you and to improve the app, and I don't share it.

## What I don't do

- No accounts or sign-up with me.
- No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services from other companies.
- No selling or sharing of your data.
- No tracking across other apps or websites.

## Deleting your data

In the app, go to **Profile → Settings → Erase all** to delete your progress on all your devices. Deleting the app removes the data stored on that device; data in your iCloud can also be managed in your device's iCloud settings. Game Center data is managed by Apple in Settings → Game Center.

## Children

KodeBuddy is a general-audience learning app. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
