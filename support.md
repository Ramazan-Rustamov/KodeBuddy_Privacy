# KodeBuddy Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email **kodebuddy.support@gmail.com**, or tap **Profile → Settings → Send feedback** in the app.

## Common questions

**How do I restore my subscription on a new device?**
Open the paywall (tap any Pro item, or Profile → Get Pro) and tap **Restore Purchases**. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

**How do I cancel KodeBuddy Pro?**
Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → KodeBuddy Pro, or Profile → KodeBuddy Pro in the app.

**My progress isn't on my other device.**
Make sure both devices are signed in to the same iCloud account with iCloud Drive turned on, then open the app on both.
